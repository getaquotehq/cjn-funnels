Questions

Common questions

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Your quote is free and there's no obligation to go ahead. We'll give you honest advice and the numbers, and you decide.

Can I add a battery to my existing solar?

Often, yes. We'll check your current system and let you know which battery options work with it.

Are there rebates for batteries?

Yes. The federal Cheaper Home Batteries program reduces the upfront cost of eligible batteries, and eligible solar systems attract STC incentives too. Any rebate you're eligible for is applied in your quote.

What does the 10 year workmanship warranty cover?

It covers the quality of our installation for 10 years. Panels, inverters and batteries also carry their own manufacturer warranties, which we'll explain in your quote.

Which areas do you service?

We're based in Southport and install across the Gold Coast and South East Queensland.
